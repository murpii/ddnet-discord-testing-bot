# Privacy Policy for the DDNet Map Testing Bot

**Last updated:** June 20, 2026 \
**Contact:** https://ddnet.org/staff/#contact

This is what the map testing bot does with your data on the DDNet Discord server. It
only covers the bot itself. Whatever Discord does with your data is covered by
Discord's own Privacy Policy.

The bot has no database and isn't built to collect personal data. It only uses what
Discord already provides, and only to run the map testing process, where mappers
submit maps and testers review them. We keep that data because we have a genuine
interest in running testing and keeping a record of how each map was decided. We never
sell it or use it for ads.

## What it keeps

Everything the bot handles comes straight from Discord and is only there to run
testing.

- Your Discord username, display name, ID, avatar and roles, so it knows who did what.
- The messages in the testing channels, so it can follow the workflow.
- A simple tally of tester activity, such as ready and decline votes, and a per-map
  changelog of what was decided, by whom, and when.

## Test logs are public

When a map channel wraps up, the bot saves a "test log" of that channel and publishes
it on the DDNet website at https://ddnet.org/testlogs/, where anyone can read and
download it. That log includes the messages, the names and avatars of everyone who
took part, and the files shared in the channel (the `.map` file, screenshots, videos,
and so on).

If you join a testing channel, treat what you post there as part of a public,
permanent record of how the map was tested. The logs exist so that anyone can look
back and see how a map made it to release.

Your data otherwise only goes two places. It stays in Discord, where the bot lives,
and on the DDNet website, where the logs are hosted.

## How long it sticks around

There's no database, just files. Tester scores, changelogs and saved test logs stay
around for as long as they're useful as a record of testing, and the public logs on
the website are meant to be permanent.

## Your rights, and how to reach us

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct or delete the data the bot
holds about you, or raise a concern with your local data protection authority. Reach
out at https://ddnet.org/staff/#contact. Anything already published in a public test
log is part of a permanent record of how a map was tested, so we may not be able to
take it back out.
